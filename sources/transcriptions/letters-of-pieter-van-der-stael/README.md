# The Van der Stael Letters

**Church correspondence from the Cape of Good Hope, 1656–1664**

---

Pieter van der Stael arrived at the Cape of Good Hope with his wife and children on **5 March 1656** to begin his service as *sieckentrooster* (sick-comforter). He replaced his cousin **Willem Barentsz Wijlant**, who departed for Batavia five days later on the tenth of March.

Among the duties of the *sieckentrooster* was to report regularly on church matters to the **Classis Amsterdam** — the regional church assembly in the Dutch Republic responsible for oversight of Reformed congregations in VOC territories.

---

## The Letters

The letters from Pieter van der Stael to the Classis Amsterdam cover the period **6 April 1652** to **14 May 1663**. The first letter covers activities from 6 April 1652 to April 1655 and was recorded by his predecessor. His last letter was dated 14 May 1663, when he was on the point of departing for Batavia, hoping to join his brother-in-law, **Jan van Riebeeck**, on Malacca.

A total of **nine letters** sent by him are found in the archives, covering pages 1 through 37 and numbered as nos. 1 through 9. From the letters themselves it became clear that at least ten letters were sent, but the one between number three and number four is missing. There is also an indication that another letter was sent to the Classis in 1664, but no trace of it has yet been found — it has been given a provisional number of **no. 3bis**.

In the following transcripts, the letters are numbered 1 to 9 and ordered by the date signed (or, in the case of the missing letter, by the date sent).

### The Tenth Letter

In addition to the above-mentioned letters, a tenth letter is included in this series. This letter is strictly speaking not part of the "letters from the Cape" (inventory numbers 206–208), but belongs to the letters from East India and comes from inventory number 186: *"Ingekomen stukken betreffende kerkelijke zaken in Oost-Indië, 1655 april – 1682 augustus 13"*, numbered as **no. 118**.

This letter was Pieter van der Stael's last report of activities concerning the church and spiritual welfare at the Cape, written after he and his family arrived in Batavia on **16 November 1663**. The letter is dated **20 January 1664** and also contains a verbatim copy of a letter/statement made by his successor, **Ernestus Back**.
