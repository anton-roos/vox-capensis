Cape Muster Roll 1695 - Knechts
The muster roll given here is that of so-called Free Servants (vrij knechten) residing in the household of others, towards the end of 1695. The date of compilation is given as 31 December 1695. The heads of household where they are residing are not given. The list is divided into the three districts, Stellenbosch, Drakenstein and the Cape district. This list was transcribed from the Cape Archives VC 39, volume 1, pages 174 to 177.
