Cape Muster Roll 1700 - Free Men
The muster roll transcribed here is that of the Free Settlers of the year 1700 from the Cape Archives VC 39, volume 1, pages 192 to 208. No date of compilation is noted. The head of household and the names of wives are given but children are metioned. The roll is divided into two districts, Stellenbosch and followed by the Cape district on page 201. No distinction is made between the inhabitants of Stellenbosch and Drakenstein. By comparing the names of the inhabitants to those of other years, it can be deduced where the list of inhabitants of the Drakenstein district probably begins. It appears to be on page 196. Pages 193, 194, 195,196, 205, 206 and 207 are torn along the edges rendering some of the text unreadable.
