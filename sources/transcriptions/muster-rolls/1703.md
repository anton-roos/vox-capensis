Cape Muster Roll 1703 - Free Men
This muster roll is that of the Free Settlers at the Cape of Good Hope for the year 1703 and was transcribed from the Cape Archives VC 49, pages 42 to 63. The head of household and the names of wives are given but no children are metioned. The roll is divided into three districts; starting with Stellenbosch on page 42, Drakenstein on page 47 followed by the Cape district on page 52. The end of the roll is dated 31 December 1703.
