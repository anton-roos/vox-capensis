Cape Muster Roll 1704 - Free Men
This muster roll is that of the Free Settlers at the Cape of Good Hope for the year 1704 and was transcribed from the Cape Archives VC 49, pages 63 to 85. The head of household and the names of wives are given but no children are metioned. The roll is divided into three districts; starting with Stellenbosch on page 63, Drakenstein on page 69 followed by the Cape district on page 74. The end of the roll is dated 31 December 1704 (den laasten December 1704).
