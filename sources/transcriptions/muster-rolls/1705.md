Cape Muster Roll 1705 - Free Men
This muster roll is that of the Free Settlers at the Cape of Good Hope for the year 1705 and was transcribed from the Cape Archives VC 49, pages 85 to 107. The head of household and the names of wives are given but no children are metioned. The roll is divided into three districts; starting with Stellenbosch on page 85, Drakenstein on page 90 followed by the Cape district on page 96. The stat of the roll is dated Anno 1705. It appears that the original roll was damaged causing some text in the beginning of the roll to be either unreadable to the compilers of the VC document or lost. The lost names could be deduced from previous years as being Adam Tas, Aeltje ter Mollen, and Jan Elbertsz.
