Cape Muster Roll 1706 - Free Men
This muster roll is that of the Free Settlers at the Cape of Good Hope for the year 1706 and was transcribed from the Cape Archives VC 49, pages 107 to 127. The head of household and the names of wives are given but no children are mentioned. The roll is divided into three districts; starting with Stellenbosch on page 107, Drakenstein on page 112 followed by the Cape district on page 118. Both the start and end of the roll is dated 31 December 1706. Several entries are partially missing and replaced by the author of the copy with dots (. . . . ). This suggests that the original roll was damaged rendering portions of the text unreadable. The transcriber has attempted to reconstruct the missing portions from previous and subsequent rolls. The reconstructions have been added in (brackets and italicized) after the partial entry. These reconstructions are purely speculative and should not be taken at face value.
