Cape Muster Roll 1707 - Free Men
This muster roll is that of the Free Settlers at the Cape of Good Hope for the year 1707 and was transcribed from the Cape Archives VC 49, pages 127to 147. The head of household and the names of wives are given but no children are metioned. The roll is divided into three districts; starting with Stellenbosch on page 127, Drakenstein on page 131 followed by the Cape district on page 137. The end of the roll is dated 31 December 1707.
