Cape Muster Roll 1711 - Free Men
This muster roll is that of the Free Settlers at the Cape of Good Hope for the year 1711 and was transcribed from the Cape Archives VC 49, pages 219 to 244. The head of household and the names of wives are given but no children are metioned. However, the children for some, but not all families, were extracted by the transcriber from the original rolls at the Nationaal Archief in The Hague. In these cases the family may be follow by xs, xd, representing the number of sons and daughters. The roll is divided into three districts; starting with Stellenbosch on page 219, Drakenstein on page 225 followed by the Cape district on page 233. The end of the roll is dated 20 January 1712.
