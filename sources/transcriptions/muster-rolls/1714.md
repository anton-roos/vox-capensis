Cape Muster Roll 1714 - Free Men
This muster roll is that of the Free Settlers at the Cape of Good Hope for the year 1714 and was transcribed from the Cape Archives VC 49, pages 288 to 310. The head of household and the names of wives are given but no children are metioned. The roll is divided into three districts; starting with Stellenbosch on page 288, Drakenstein on page 293 followed by the Cape district on page 300. Both the start and end of the roll is dated 31 December 1714 (ultimo December). The population numbers appears to have recovered somewhat after disater year 1713, with the influx of new Burghers.
